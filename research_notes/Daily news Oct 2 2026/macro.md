## What is the biggest market-moving story today?

### Takeaway
The single biggest market-moving event on October 2, 2026 is the release of the US September 2026 Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP) report at 8:30 AM ET, which lands against a backdrop of 24-year-high Treasury yields (~5.28%), oil near $93/barrel, and intense debate over whether the Federal Reserve will hike rates again at its October 27–28 meeting.

### Cited Findings
- The US Bureau of Labor Statistics released the Employment Situation for September 2026 on October 2, 2026 at 8:30 AM ET (USDL-26-1435 embargoed until release) — [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/pdf/empsit.pdf)
- Consensus forecast for September NFP was 84,000–100,000 jobs (various surveys), with unemployment expected flat at 4.1% — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-26/us-jobs-report-seen-showing-90-000-payrolls-4-1-unemployment-rate); [FactSet](https://insight.factset.com/total-nonfarm-payrolls-for-september-2026-are-projected-to-rise-by-90000)
- Prior month (August 2026) NFP was 162,000, beating the 53,000 Dow Jones consensus — [BLS August release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)
- ADP private payrolls for September (released Sep 30) showed 90,000 jobs, beating 68,000 estimate — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/private-sector-jobs-rose-by-90000-in-september-better-than-expected-adp-reports.html)
- The September jobs report was the most anticipated release of the week, described as "the single most market-moving data release on the economic calendar" — [CNBC preview](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/01/the-september-jobs-report-will-be-released-friday-heres-what-to-expect.html)
- Prediction market traders were pricing a higher-than-consensus payrolls beat heading into the report — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/prediction-market-traders-september-jobs-report-will-beat-estimates.html)

### Inferences
- Given the ADP beat (90k vs 68k expected) and prediction market pricing, there was an elevated probability of the official BLS report also beating the ~84k–90k consensus, which would likely push Treasury yields higher and reduce the probability of near-term Fed rate cuts.
- A blowout print (>130k) would likely reignite October rate-hike bets (currently ~37–64% probability) and send stocks lower; a miss would relieve pressure on yields and support equities.
