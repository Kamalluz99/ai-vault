## What is the biggest Malaysia news story today?

### Takeaway
Formula 1 returns to Malaysia for the first time in nine years as Sepang International Circuit hosts the 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix, with the first practice sessions (FP1 and FP2) taking place on October 2 — the most significant sporting and national event in Malaysia on this date.

### Cited Findings
- The Formula 1 Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix is being held at Sepang International Circuit, Selangor, Malaysia from October 2–4, 2026 — [Formula 1 Official](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/formula-1-and-fia-confirm-malaysia-will-join-2026-calendar-as-host-venue-for-bahrain-grand-prix.6lL7vjFEM2VVynRHvg1TCf)
- This is the first Formula 1 race in Malaysia since 2017, a nine-year absence — [AutoBuzz.my](https://autobuzz.my/2026/07/26/formula-1-returns-to-malaysia-from-2-4-october-2026/)
- Free Practice 1 (FP1) was scheduled for 12:30 MYT and Free Practice 2 (FP2) at 16:00 MYT on October 2 — [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13590788/bahrain-gp-in-malaysia-2026-dates-schedule-weather-uk-start-time-and-how-to-watch-or-stream-f1-race-at-sepang-live-on-sky-sports)
- The race is officially the "2026 Bahrain Grand Prix" but hosted in Malaysia because Formula 1 could not hold it at Sakhir due to ongoing conflict and security concerns in the Middle East — [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13566600/malaysia-added-to-2026-f1-calendar-in-october-to-host-postponed-bahrain-gp-amid-continued-conflict-in-middle-east)
- Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim joined the Pit Lane Walk at Sepang on October 1, 2026, was received by Sepang International Circuit and Bahrain International Circuit management, and inspected final preparations — [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/01/pm-anwar-joins-sepang-pit-lane-walk-ahead-of-formula-1-bahrain-grand-prix/237277); [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/sports/news.php?id=2614262)
- PM Anwar was accompanied by Youth and Sports Minister Dr. Mohammed Taufiq Johari and Chief Secretary Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar — [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/sports/news.php?id=2614262)
- Over 200,000 spectators are expected across the race weekend (October 2–4) — [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/01/pm-anwar-joins-sepang-pit-lane-walk-ahead-of-formula-1-bahrain-grand-prix/237277)
- The race is the 16th round of the 2026 FIA Formula One World Championship — [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13590788/bahrain-gp-in-malaysia-2026-dates-schedule-weather-uk-start-time-and-how-to-watch-or-stream-f1-race-at-sepang-live-on-sky-sports)
- Weather for practice day: temperatures 23–32°C with heat index peaking at 37°C and 50% chance of precipitation — [Formula 1 Official](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-is-the-weather-forecast-for-the-2026-bahrain-grand-prix-in-malaysia.5OZ23exBdO608dxwkDLrad)
- Malaysia required only RM16 million in preparation costs to host the race at Sepang — [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/malaysia-needs-only-rm16mil-for-sepang-f1-preparations-says-anwar_7568f3)

### Inferences
- The Malaysian government's rapid mobilisation to host the displaced Bahrain GP at minimal cost (RM16 million) demonstrates Sepang's maintained readiness and Malaysia's desire to re-enter the F1 calendar permanently.
- PM Anwar's personal attendance at the pit lane walk signals the government's strong political backing for the event as a national prestige project.
- The Middle East conflict context gives Malaysia an unexpected but high-profile opportunity to showcase the country internationally during a race broadcast to a global audience.

## What happened and who are the key players?

### Takeaway
Malaysia is hosting the Bahrain Grand Prix at Sepang because the original Bahrain venue was rendered unusable by Middle East security concerns; the Malaysian government under PM Anwar Ibrahim facilitated the hosting with quick preparation and strong political endorsement.

### Cited Findings
- Sepang International Circuit, located in Selangor, is the host venue — last hosted F1 in 2017 — [AutoBuzz.my](https://autobuzz.my/2026/07/26/formula-1-returns-to-malaysia-from-2-4-october-2026/)
- Formula 1 and the FIA confirmed Malaysia's addition to the 2026 calendar to replace the cancelled Bahrain round — [Formula 1 Official](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/formula-1-and-fia-confirm-malaysia-will-join-2026-calendar-as-host-venue-for-bahrain-grand-prix.6lL7vjFEM2VVynRHvg1TCf)
- Key players: PM Anwar Ibrahim (political endorsement), Youth and Sports Minister Dr. Mohammed Taufiq Johari (government liaison), Sepang International Circuit management, Bahrain International Circuit management — [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/sports/news.php?id=2614262)
- George Russell has been previewed as a front-runner based on recent championship form — [F1 Fansite](https://www.f1-fansite.com/f1-news/previewing-the-2026-bahrain-grand-prix-in-malaysia-back-to-back-for-russell/)
