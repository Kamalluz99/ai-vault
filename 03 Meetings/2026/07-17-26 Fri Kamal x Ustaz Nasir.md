> [!info] Phone call with [[Ustaz Nasir]] on the full step-by-step process for a Singaporean Muslim to register and solemnise a marriage in Selangor. Kamal is based in Singapore; partner lives at Jalan Tun Razak, KL; wedding venue is [[Gazi House]], Hulu Langat.

## Summary

Ustaz Nasir outlined the complete documentation and administrative process. The order of steps matters — some documents must precede others (e.g. Embassy letter before Putrajaya endorsement).

### Step-by-step process

1. **Settle Singapore documents**
   - Ensure passport and visa are valid
   - Obtain the **MUIS single/bachelor approval letter** from [[MUIS]] (Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura) — confirms status as unmarried

2. **Travel to Malaysia** on a social visit pass

3. **Singapore High Commission, Kuala Lumpur (Embassy)**
   - Obtain the **"No Objection to Marriage" letter** (Surat Tiada Halangan Perkahwinan) — do this **before** Putrajaya

4. **Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Putrajaya — Bahagian WISA (Visa section)**
   - Bring the Embassy letter here to be endorsed
   - Cannot go to Putrajaya first — the endorsement requires the Embassy document

5. **Immigration Department (KL or Putrajaya)**
   - Obtain **"Surat Pengesahan Pas bagi Tujuan Perkahwinan Warga Negara Asing"** (Confirmation of Pass for Marriage Purposes — Foreign National)
   - Must appear in **person** — cannot be delegated to anyone else

6. **HIV test** — can be done in Malaysia
   - Gets a referral letter printed from SPPIM system later; test must be done within 3 months of wedding

7. **Partner inputs data into SPPIM system**
   - Partner at KL enters both parties' details into SPPIM
   - For permanent address: enter **Singapore address**; for current address: can use partner's Malaysia address
   - System generates HIV referral letters and printable forms for both

8. **Visit nearest mosque with wali present**
   - Recommended mosques near Jalan Tun Razak: [[Masjid Benua Abbas]] or [[Masjid As-Syafiin]] (near KLCC)
   - Imam/jurunikah signs documents; wali must attend
   - Bring **all** documents without exception — nothing can be missing

9. **Submit to JAWI** (Jabatan Agama Islam Wilayah Persekutuan)
   - JAWI issues **"Kebenaran Berkahwin Luar Negeri"** (Permission to Marry Abroad)
   - Since wedding is in Hulu Langat, JAWI routes this to **Pendaftar Perkahwinan Hulu Langat**

10. **Pejabat Agama Hulu Langat**
    - Bring JAWI documents here to be registered and matched
    - Receive **Catatan Daftar Perkahwinan** (marriage registration certificate)
    - Pass this to [[Gazi House]] (the wedding venue)

11. **Wedding at [[Gazi House]], Hulu Langat**
    - Gazi House has its own jurunikah (solemniser) on site
    - If unsure who can solemnise, ask Pejabat Agama Hulu Langat directly

### Timeline

| Milestone | Deadline |
|-----------|----------|
| Begin entire process | At least **3 months** before wedding |
| HIV test | Within **3 months** before wedding (not earlier) |
| All documentation settled | At least **7 days** before akad nikah |

> [!warning] Don't start more than ~6 months early — some documents expire. 3 months is the sweet spot. Hard deadline: everything done **7 days before akad nikah**.

### Key document checklist

- [ ] Valid passport + visa
- [ ] MUIS single/bachelor approval letter
- [ ] No Objection to Marriage letter (Singapore High Commission, KL)
- [ ] Endorsement from Kementerian Luar, Putrajaya (Bahagian WISA)
- [ ] Surat Pengesahan Pas bagi Tujuan Perkahwinan Warga Negara Asing (Immigration)
- [ ] HIV test result (both parties)
- [ ] SPPIM printout (partner handles)
- [ ] JAWI Kebenaran Berkahwin Luar Negeri
- [ ] Catatan Daftar Perkahwinan (from Pejabat Agama Hulu Langat)
